# Aetherstore Engine - Accelerated Go-To-Market Roadmap

## Executive Summary

This roadmap outlines an accelerated go-to-market strategy for Aetherstore Engine that leverages existing capabilities to achieve faster market entry while maintaining a path for future enhancements. By focusing on a phased approach that removes dependencies on external factors like the IWSDK, we can bring the product to market in 3-6 months instead of waiting 9-16 months for full feature completion.

## Strategic Approach

### Current State
- ✅ Core platform is functionally complete
- ✅ 3D rendering and virtual try-on work with WebXR fallbacks
- ✅ AI personalization and recommendation engines are operational
- ❌ Waiting for Meta's IWSDK packages for full VR/AR capabilities

### Opportunity
Launch with current capabilities and progressively enhance with IWSDK when available, rather than delaying the entire launch.

## Risk Mitigation

### Technical Risks
**Risk**: Performance issues with 3D rendering on lower-end devices
**Mitigation**: Implement progressive loading and quality scaling

**Risk**: Security vulnerabilities in early release
**Mitigation**: Comprehensive penetration testing and security audits

### Market Risks
**Risk**: Slow adoption due to unfamiliarity with 3D commerce
**Mitigation**: Extensive education campaigns and clear ROI demonstration

**Risk**: Competition from established players
**Mitigation**: Focus on unique features and superior user experience

### Dependency Risks
**Risk**: Delay in IWSDK release affects VR/AR roadmap
**Mitigation**: Proceed with WebXR fallbacks and progressive enhancement
