# Roadmap: TRIBE v2 & Neuro-Fashion Integration

## 📌 Overview
[TRIBE v2](https://github.com/facebookresearch/tribev2) is a "Foundation Model of Vision, Audition, and Language for In-Silico Neuroscience." It predicts fMRI brain responses to multimodal stimuli (video, audio, text), mapping how the human brain processes complex sensory information.

## 🚀 Why TRIBE v2 for AetherStore?

If **SHIFT15M** predicts *what* people like based on data, **TRIBE v2** predicts *how* they feel based on neural patterns. This opens the door to **Neuro-Fashion**:

*   **Aesthetic Response Prediction:** Simulate how a specific color palette or fabric texture (audio/visual) activates reward centers in the brain.
*   **Attention Mapping:** Predict which parts of a 3D garment will draw the most neural attention before it's even manufactured.
*   **Emotional Branding:** Analyze the "neural vibe" of a digital store's music (audition), lighting (vision), and product descriptions (language).
*   **In-Silico Focus Groups:** Run thousands of design iterations through the TRIBE v2 model to find the one that triggers the most positive "predicted" brain response.

## 🛠 Potential Integration

### 1. The "Neural Stylist"
An upgrade to the AI Stylist that suggests outfits not just based on trends, but on their "predicted" emotional impact.

### 2. Neuro-Design Dashboard
A tool for designers to upload a 3D model and see a "Brain Response Heatmap" showing how a user might perceive the garment's visual and tactile properties.

### 3. Multisensory Storefronts
Optimizing the audio-visual experience of a 3D store by predicting the combined neural response to background music and product visuals.
