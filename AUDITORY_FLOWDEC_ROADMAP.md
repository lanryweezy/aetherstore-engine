# Roadmap: FlowDec & Auditory Immersion Integration

## 📌 Overview
[FlowDec](https://github.com/facebookresearch/FlowDec) is a neural full-band audio codec (48 kHz) designed for general audio. It achieves high perceptual quality at extremely low bitrates (7.5 kbps / 4.5 kbps) using conditional flow matching.

## 🚀 Why FlowDec for AetherStore?

In a high-end 3D fashion platform, visuals are only half the experience. FlowDec provides the "Auditory Intelligence" needed for true immersion:

*   **Low-Bandwidth High-Fidelity:** Stream studio-quality background music and ambient sounds without consuming the bandwidth needed for 3D model textures.
*   **Fabric Foley:** Deliver realistic sounds of specific fabrics (the "swish" of silk, the "crinkle" of stiff denim) as the avatar moves, enhancing the tactile feel of digital clothing.
*   **Spatial Voice Chat:** Enable crystal-clear voice communication for social shopping rooms at a fraction of the data cost of standard VoIP.
*   **Virtual Runway Events:** Sync high-fidelity audio with complex 3D animations for digital fashion shows, ensuring the music never drops in quality even on mobile connections.

## 🛠 Potential Integration

### 1. The "Tactile Audio" Engine
Integrate FlowDec with the **Momentum** motion tracker. As the avatar moves, the engine triggers high-fidelity fabric sound samples compressed with FlowDec, making the digital clothes "sound" as real as they look.

### 2. Low-Latency Social Rooms
Use FlowDec for the voice layer in social shopping sessions. This ensures that even in a crowded 3D store with multiple users, the audio remains clear and the 3D performance stays high.

### 3. Adaptive Ambient Soundscapes
Automatically adjust store music quality based on the user's connection speed, using FlowDec's low-bitrate efficiency to ensure the "vibe" is never broken by buffering.
